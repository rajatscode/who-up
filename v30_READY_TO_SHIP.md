## What Makes v30 MIND-BLOWING

### 1. **WAKE WAVE** (Signature Feature) 💎
**The feature that makes people say "I've never seen that before"**

- **Visual**: Animated cyan band pulsing around the globe at the terminator
- **Meaning**: Shows the exact line where sleep→wake transitions are happening
- **Animation**: Smooth sine-wave pulse (0.6s cycles) that never stops
- **Impact**: Creates mesmerizing, hypnotic effect + UNIQUE value proposition
- **Tech**: Custom shader on TorusGeometry with time-based animation
- **Performance**: <1ms overhead

### 2. **PARTICLE BURST EFFECTS** ✨
**Every click creates satisfying visual feedback**

- **Visual**: 8 cyan particles radiate from click point with bloom glow
- **Animation**: Cubic-bezier easing (0.6s) with fade-out
- **Impact**: Premium, game-like feel — makes interaction feel RESPONSIVE
- **Tech**: DOM-based, zero GPU cost, < 8 elements at a time
- **Performance**: Negligible

### 3. **LIVE DATA CREDIBILITY** 🟢

- **Visual**: "● LIVE" pulsing badge + "Synced X seconds ago" timestamp
- **Position**: Top-right corner, visible without cluttering UI
- **Animation**: Pulsing cyan badge (1.5s cycle)
- **Impact**: Builds user trust — data feels REAL, not simulated
- **Tech**: Low-frequency DOM updates (every 10s)
