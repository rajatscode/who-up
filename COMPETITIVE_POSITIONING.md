## WHAT EACH COMPETITOR DOES

### Google Earth
**Strength**: Detailed satellite imagery, familiar interface  
**Weakness**: Static imagery, slow to load, requires significant resources  
**User Goal**: "Show me my house from space" or "Explore a city"  
**NOT a competitor for who-up's use case**

---

### NASA Worldview
**Strength**: 1,200+ real-time satellite layers (weather, sea ice, wildfire smoke)  
**Weakness**: Complex, scientific focus, not consumer-friendly, no awakeness data  
**User Goal**: "Monitor environmental conditions and changes"  
**Overlap**: Real-time, globe, interactive  
**Difference**: who-up is about *human behavior*, not *environmental science*

---

### earth.nullschool.net
**Strength**: Beautiful real-time wind/weather/ocean visualization, mesmerizing  
**Weakness**: 2D projection, no interactive regions, no human data  
**User Goal**: "See live weather patterns and ocean conditions"  
**Overlap**: Real-time, animated, beautiful  
**Difference**: who-up shows *human patterns*, not *weather patterns*

---

### Hillarys Sleep Loss Map
**Strength**: Real sleep data (Twitter mentions), actual human behavior  
**Weakness**: Social media bias, no 3D, no time-lapse, point-in-time snapshot  
**User Goal**: "See where people are struggling to sleep right now"  
**Overlap**: Sleep/awakeness focus, real-time, global  
**Difference**: who-up uses **modeled data** (more accurate than Twitter bias) + **3D visualization** + **animated wake wave** + **time-lapse**

## WHY WHO-UP WINS

### 1. **NOVELTY: Wake Wave is Genuinely Unique**

No other product animates the sleep-to-wake boundary on a 3D globe.

- Terminator visualizations exist (but show geophysics, not behavior)
- Sleep maps exist (but show social media mentions, not true patterns)
- Real-time globes exist (but show weather/satellites, not humans)

**who-up combines all three and adds animation.**

---

### 2. **DATA QUALITY: Better Than Social Media**

Hillarys' map uses Twitter data. Problem: **massive bias**.

- Twitter users are non-representative (age, geography, culture)
- "Can't sleep" tweets spike during political events, not actual insomnia
- China and Russia underrepresented (Twitter suppressed)
- Silent majority never tweets about sleep

**who-up uses population-weighted sleep curves** — scientifically calibrated, globally representative.

---

### 3. **VISUAL IMPACT: Premium Over Plain**

NASA Worldview is powerful but clinical. who-up is *beautiful*.

- Aggressive bloom effects (Three.js UnrealBloomPass at 2.0 strength)
- Cinematic color grading (deep navy background, vivid cyan wake wave)
- Interactive feedback (particle bursts on click)
- Smooth animations (spring-based transitions)

**User reaction difference**:
- NASA: "That's informative" ✅
- who-up: "That's beautiful AND informative" ✅✅

---

### 4. **TIME DIMENSION: Discovery Through Motion**

Static maps show a moment. who-up shows 24 hours.

- **Time-lapse at 1000x**: Watch wake wave sweep around Earth
- **See patterns emerge**: When do regions peak? Where are the gradual vs sharp transitions?
- **Discover regional variation**: East Asia, Western Europe, Americas have different profiles

Judges will say: "I can see the global pattern now" (they can't with static maps)

---

### 5. **INTERACTION: Every Click Feels Premium**

Google Earth: You can zoom, but it's clunky  
NASA: Mostly read-only  
Hillarys: Mostly read-only  
**who-up**: Every click triggers particles + panel + camera animation  

Judges will say: "This feels like a real product" (not a demo)
