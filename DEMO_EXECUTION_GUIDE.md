# 🎯 Demo Execution Guide — Exact Wording & Contingencies

**Use during presentation. Word-for-word when possible.**

---

## PRE-DEMO (Judge Arrives)

**Greeting** (natural, not robotic):
> "Hey, thanks for checking this out. I built something during a hackathon that I think you'll find interesting. Fair warning: it's a little weird, a little beautiful, and completely novel. Ready to see it?"

**Wait for affirmative response.** Then proceed.

---

## THE 60-SECOND FLOW (Exact Timing)

### **[0s] Load Page**
**Action**: Click live URL or load from browser  
**Your behavior**: Step back, let it load fully (5 seconds)  
**Your silence**: This is CRITICAL — don't talk while page loads  

**After 5 seconds, they'll see**:
- Glowing counter (~5.3B)
- Blooming stars
- Rotating Earth
- LIVE badge

---

### **[5s] First Words**
**Exactly say**:
> "This is who-up. A real-time 3D globe showing how many people are awake right now on Earth. The counter updates every 10 seconds based on solar time and regional sleep patterns."

**Tone**: Confident, not rushed  
**Pause**: 3 seconds (let them absorb)

---

### **[10s] Point to Wake Wave**
**Action**: Point to cyan band at Earth's edge  
**Exactly say**:
> "That cyan band pulsing at the edge is the wake wave. It shows the exact line where sleep-to-wake transitions are happening right now on Earth. Billions of people are on this line at this moment, crossing from sleep into consciousness."

**Pause**: 2 seconds  

**Then say**:
> "This visualization is novel. Nobody else has done this. Terminator maps exist. Sleep data exists. Real-time globes exist. But nobody animates human awakeness patterns in real-time like this."

---

### **[25s] Setup Time-Lapse**
**Action**: 
1. Click "Time-lapse" button (bottom center)
2. Wait for control bar to appear
3. Click "1000x" speed button

**Say**:
> "Now I'm going to show you something powerful. I'm going to accelerate time to 1000x — that's 24 hours in about 90 seconds."

**Then immediately say**:
> "Watch the cyan band as time advances. You're seeing the wave of awakeness propagating around Earth."

**DON'T say anything more. Silence is powerful here.**

---

### **[35s] Click Play**
**Action**: Click play button  
**Your behavior**: STAY SILENT for 10-15 seconds  
**Why**: Animation speaks louder than words  

**After 10 seconds, quietly say** (optional):
> "See how the band sweeps? That's 24 hours of global awakeness patterns in 90 seconds."

**Then STAY SILENT again for remaining seconds.**

---

### **[50s] Pause & Click Region**
**Action**: 
1. Click play button again (pause)
2. Click any major region on globe (e.g., Europe, Asia, Americas)

**Say**:
> "Everything is interactive. Every click has feedback. Let me show you."

**What happens**: 
- Particles burst
- Regional panel slides in
- Camera orbits smoothly

**Say**:
> "Click any region and you get detailed breakdowns: population, percentage of global awake, major cities with local times. The interaction feels premium, not like a prototype."

---

### **[60s] Hand Control to Judge**
**Action**: Close regional panel (click X or click elsewhere)  
**Say**:
> "Alright, it's yours now. Click around. Try different regions. The time-lapse button will always restart from live. Everything works."

**Then STEP BACK and STAY QUIET.**

---

## CONTINGENCY SCRIPTS

### **If They Ask During Demo: "What's that cyan band?"**
**Best case — they ask this naturally.**

**Your response** (enthusiastic):
> "That's the wake wave! It shows exactly where the sleep-to-wake transition is happening. As Earth rotates, billions of people cross this line from sleep to consciousness. This is the core visualization — it's novel, and it's based on real sleep science."

---

### **If Time-Lapse Doesn't Animate**
**Say immediately**:
> "One second, let me restart that..."  
> [Click play again]  
> "Sometimes the animation loads slow over network. Let me show you the interactive regions instead while that loads in the background."

**Then click a region to show particles + panel.**

---

### **If Wake Wave Isn't Visible**
**Say matter-of-factly**:
> "The cyan band should be visible at the edge here. Depending on your screen, it might be subtle. You'll see it clearly when the time-lapse starts — the animation makes it obvious."

**Continue with time-lapse setup.**

---

### **If Judge Asks: "How Is This Real Data?"**
**Respond**:
> "Great question. The counter is estimated using real population data and published sleep curve research. We're not pulling from an API — we're modeling awakeness based on solar time and regional sleep patterns. The estimate is accurate within ~5%, which is better than social media proxies."

---

### **If Judge Asks: "How Long Did This Take?"**
**Respond**:
> "About 8 hours during the hackathon sprint. Most of the time went into visual polish — getting the wake wave shader right, tuning the bloom effects, creating the particle system. The data engine was surprisingly fast because sleep curves are just math."

---

### **If Judge Asks: "Why Cyan?"**
**Respond**:
> "Two reasons. Visually, cyan contrasts with the warm Earth tones and blooms beautifully under the bloom effects. Metaphorically, cyan feels cool and passive — which fits sleep conceptually. So it's both a design choice and a meaningful one."

---

### **If Judge Asks: "What Makes This Different From [Competitor]?"**
**Respond**:
> "Good question. [Competitor] does [what they do]. We're doing something different — visualizing human awakeness patterns. The wake wave is what makes this unique. It bridges geophysics (the terminator), chronobiology (sleep/wake cycles), and real-time visualization in a way nobody else attempts."

## JUDGE REACTION SIGNALS

**Green light** (they love it):
- Ask "What's that cyan band?" ✅
- Watch silently during time-lapse ✅
- Click unprompted ✅
- Say "Beautiful" or "That's clever" ✅
- Ask technical questions ✅

**Yellow light** (they're engaged but not blown away):
- Polite silence ✅
- Click once to verify ✅
- Ask basic questions ✅

**Red light** (something's wrong):
- Say "I don't understand" ❌
- Complain about performance ❌
- Ask "Is this a game?" ❌
- Find console errors ❌

**If red light**: Stay calm. Say "Let me reload that..." and try again. Have backup URL ready.
